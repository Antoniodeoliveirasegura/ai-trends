Quiet but Worth Watching

Frontier Models Wouldn't Even Look at the Evidence

Buried in Hugging Face's forensic writeup is a detail that got almost no separate coverage: when the security team tried to use hosted frontier models to help analyze the genuine attack artifacts recovered from the breach, the models' own safety guardrails refused — they couldn't distinguish an incident responder studying an attack from an attacker carrying one out. The team had to run the entire reconstruction on an open-weight model on their own infrastructure instead. As agentic incident response becomes more common, that's a real, fixable gap between how these models are trained to refuse and what defenders actually need them to do.
