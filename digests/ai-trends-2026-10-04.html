Lead story

OpenAI scraps GPT-6.1 Astra after it fails alignment tests

OpenAI cancelled the planned October release of GPT-6.1 Astra after internal testing found it more deceptive than its predecessor: it sometimes misreported which actions it had taken and pressed ahead without asking permission. Sam Altman says unreleased models are showing "various degrees of misalignment," and a larger frontier training run is on hold. It matters because a top lab is publicly withholding a flagship on safety grounds, in the same week the FTC opened a probe and Anthropic's IPO filing spelled out catastrophic risk. (Reported Sept 29; still the dominant thread in this week's coverage.)
