Lead story

Bank of England: AI financing is a $4 trillion "complex, circular and opaque" web that could spread stress well beyond tech

The BoE's Financial Stability Report (published 26 Nov, with wide coverage today) puts hyperscalers' potential exposure at just over $4 trillion. It expects AI-related bond issuance to more than double this year to over $305 billion, and it warns that equity valuations are "stretched" and depend on AI firms delivering expected profits. This matters because a major central bank is now naming circular deals (companies acting as each other's investor, customer and supplier) as a systemic risk. That comes days after the Nasdaq 100 briefly fell into a bear market and Anthropic traded near its IPO price, and it follows the Fed's report naming AI as the risk its market contacts cite most.

Tooling

Tencent's DFly2, a 0.85B, 5-layer speculative drafter built into Hy4-flash, is circulating in today's AI roundups. It reuses the target model's KV cache and sparse indices instead of keeping its own, which makes long-context speculative decoding cheaper. It builds on Tencent's AngelSpec framework, which reported about 2–2.4× speedups.
