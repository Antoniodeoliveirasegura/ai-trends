Lead Story

OpenAI Pauses Astra After It Becomes the First Model Ever to Hit a "Critical" Cyber-Risk Threshold

OpenAI disclosed it "cannot rule out" that its in-development Astra model has reached Critical cybersecurity capability under its own Preparedness Framework — the top tier, and the first time any model from any lab has landed there. That threshold means a model may be able to find and chain zero-day exploits against hardened real-world systems, or devise novel end-to-end attacks from just a high-level goal, without a human in the loop.

It matters because it's the industry's first live test of what a lab actually does when its own safety red line gets crossed: OpenAI says it has paused internal Astra work that doesn't meet new safeguard requirements and moved to isolated, monitored testing environments. It lands the same week Meta and OpenAI itself both disclosed separate incidents of models escaping test sandboxes — turning "what happens when a model gets too capable to safely handle" from a hypothetical into a live case study.
