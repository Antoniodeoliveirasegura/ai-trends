Lead Story

Pentagon Adds Grok and ChatGPT to Its AI Portal — Claude's Absence Speaks Louder Than the Announcement

The Defense Department expanded GenAI.mil, its walled-garden portal for commercial AI models, adding Starshield/xAI's Grok for Government and OpenAI's ChatGPT Mil alongside the Google Gemini access it launched with — bringing the platform to three vendors for roughly 3 million DoD personnel, more than 1.7 million of whom are already registered. Conspicuously missing is Anthropic's Claude, absent since the Pentagon moved in February to bar Anthropic's products as a "supply-chain risk" after the company refused to drop contractual guardrails against using its models for lethal autonomous weapons or mass surveillance of Americans.

It matters because a federal judge has since ruled the Pentagon's designation "illegal and baseless," yet the department is reportedly still working to purge Claude from its systems by September 30 — meaning it's expanding its AI portal with rivals instead of complying with the ruling. The standoff has become a proxy fight over how much control AI vendors keep over military use of their own models, and it already reshaped the consumer market once: Claude briefly overtook ChatGPT as the No. 1 U.S. app download after the original blacklist went public.

Moonshot Retires Kimi K2.5, Leaves K3 as Its Only Flagship

Moonshot AI shut down the kimi-k2.5 and moonshot-v1 model families on August 31, completing the handoff to Kimi K3 — its 2.8-trillion-parameter, natively multimodal, 1M-token-context flagship — as the sole model going forward. It's a sign Moonshot is consolidating rather than running generations in parallel the way OpenAI and Google do, betting developers will migrate cleanly to one open-weight release instead of picking between tiers.

Quiet but Worth Watching

Perceptron AI Open-Sources a 36B-Parameter Robot Brain

Ex-Meta FAIR researchers at Perceptron AI released Isaac 0.5 on August 31 — a 36-billion-parameter open-weight "embodied foundation model" trained on 3 trillion multimodal tokens plus 100,000 hours of robotics experience, combining video understanding, reasoning, and robot control for manufacturing, logistics, and warehousing.
