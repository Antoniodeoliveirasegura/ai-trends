Lead story

White House gates who can test frontier models first

The White House (via the Office of the National Cyber Director) asked OpenAI and Anthropic to put their newest models through a US government review before sharing them with the UK's AI Security Institute. Anthropic has complied so far, keeping Claude Mythos 5.1 with US organizations. It matters because pre-release safety testing, until now a loose international norm, is becoming a controlled national-security chokepoint. Today's roundups report access was restored after a reversal; I could not confirm that.
