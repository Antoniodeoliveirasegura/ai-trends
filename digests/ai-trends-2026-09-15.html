Cloudflare Starts Blocking "Mixed-Use" AI Crawlers by Default Today

As of today, any Cloudflare-protected site running ads blocks bots that blend search indexing, agent retrieval, and model training by default, unless the site owner opts out. The policy — announced in July — formalizes a three-way taxonomy (search, agent, training) and is meant to pressure AI companies into splitting their crawlers apart instead of running one bot that does all three under a search-engine label. It applies to new customers, new sites, and all existing free-tier accounts immediately.

A New Training Trick Attacks the GPU-Idling Problem in Agent Reinforcement Learning

FlashREINFORCE, trending on alphaXiv this week, drops the critic and the group-wise sampling most agentic RL pipelines rely on in favor of critic-free, single-rollout updates with trajectory-level trust screening and length-normalized rewards. The target is a specific, expensive failure mode: in coding and agent workloads, short rollouts finish fast while long ones straggle, leaving large chunks of a GPU cluster sitting idle waiting for synchronization.
