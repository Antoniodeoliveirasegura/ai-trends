Lead story

Anthropic's IPO is reportedly oversubscribed several times over on its first days of marketing

Bloomberg-sourced reports from Nov 11–12 say orders already cover the shares on offer several times, led by large anchor-size orders. That is despite last week's pushback from investors after the Nov 5 tech selloff. With the $70–78 range and the strategic private placement, filings point to as much as $90.4B raised, and some reports say underwriters are considering a valuation above $1.5T. Strong demand would mean public markets accept a frontier lab at a trillion-plus valuation. That outcome would affect OpenAI's $1.25T private round and the wider AI-debt debate. Next step: Dario Amodei and Jamie Dimon hold a live-streamed investor event on Nov 13. The price and listing date (Nasdaq: ANT) have not been announced.

Tooling & Safety

OpenAI's "Control Room" kill switch now covers every research inference workload. Inference engines check with it before each request, so it can halt model output within 35 seconds of a decision to intervene. If Control Room itself goes down, output stops (it fails closed). Critics note that OpenAI has not said whether 35s is a worst case or an average.

"AI usage generates a fragile codebase" was the most-discussed AI thread on Hacker News this week. Commenters cited GitClear-style data: code duplication up about 81%, refactoring down about 70%, and DORA's finding that 25% more AI use goes with 7.2% lower delivery stability.
