Lead story

Anthropic files S-1 for a Nasdaq IPO under ticker “ANT”

Anthropic filed a Form S-1 on Oct 26 to list Class A shares on the Nasdaq Global Select Market, with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley among the underwriters. Price range and share count are still blank, but the filing discloses a founder-controlled structure (50.1% of voting power) and huge compute commitments (at least $110B with Amazon through April 2036, $120B with SpaceX through 2029). It is the first public look at the books of a frontier lab last valued at $965B (May 2026), and it sets the benchmark for how markets price the AI race. Reported financials conflict across outlets (one cites an $80B H1 loss, another $2.7B profit), so check the filing itself.

Discussed: financial and trade press, widespread; ongoing as analysts dig into the prospectus. Filed Oct 26, so it is not new today, but coverage is still building.

Credential-sharing with agents as a security norm
