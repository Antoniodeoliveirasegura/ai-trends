GPT-6 Astra Cracks an 85-Year-Old Enigma Message in 10 Hours

Bloomberg developer Carter Leffen used GPT-6 Astra to decrypt a German Army Enigma message from July 10, 1941 that had resisted every attempt since 2005. Astra spotted the repeated place-name "Rosenow" from a related, already-solved telegram, used it to shrink the keyspace, then wrote its own Enigma simulator and parallel key-search program to crack the remaining 82-letter cipher — a mundane marching-orders request, but a striking demonstration of a model self-tooling its way through a decades-old cryptanalysis problem.
