Lead story

The AI mega-listing race tightens: Anthropic's IPO window and OpenAI's $1.4T bridge round

Reporting this week keeps converging on the same picture: Anthropic is targeting a public valuation above $2 trillion (vs. a $965B private mark), with its roadshow reportedly slipping to around the week of Nov. 9 or after the Nov. 3 midterms, while OpenAI is reported to be seeking at least $30B at about $1.4T as a bridge after pushing its own IPO past 2026. Together they would be the largest AI capital events ever and set the price of the whole sector. Caveat: timing and loss figures conflict across outlets, and several pieces are from late September; no public S-1 was found.
