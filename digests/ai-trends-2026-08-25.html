Google Hands Its A2A Protocol to the Same Neutral Foundation That Now Also Holds MCP

Google formally transferred its Agent2Agent (A2A) protocol to the Linux Foundation-backed Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF), placing the two dominant agent standards — A2A for agent-to-agent handoffs, and Anthropic's MCP for agent-to-tool access — under one neutral governance body.
