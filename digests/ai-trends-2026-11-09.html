Lead story · 2 Nov

NYT and Tribune papers sue Brave over scraped index reportedly resold to Anthropic

The New York Times and three Tribune Publishing papers sued Brave Software in Manhattan federal court on November 2, alleging a scraping index was resold to Anthropic. It extends the copyright fight from model makers to the data suppliers behind them, a path that could expose the whole training-data supply chain. Discussion venue and engagement not measured.
