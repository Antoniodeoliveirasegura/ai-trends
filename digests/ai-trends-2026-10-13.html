Lead story

Suspected AI-driven hacks hit seven South Korean financial firms

President Lee Jae Myung told a cabinet meeting there are “signs that artificial intelligence was used” in attacks on seven institutions (Shinhan, KB Kookmin, Hana, BNK Busan, Yegaram, Welcome, Hyundai Capital) that exposed data on 68,000+ people per the Financial Services Commission. Officials suspect ARTEX, an LLM-powered penetration-testing framework, with entry via loan-recruiter lookup and sales-support systems. It matters because it is one of the first government-level statements tying agentic offensive tooling to a real multi-bank breach. AI involvement is still suspected, not confirmed; police are investigating.
