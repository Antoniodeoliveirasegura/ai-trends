Lead story

FTC sends 50-page civil investigative demands to OpenAI and Anthropic

The FTC reportedly issued civil investigative demands on Oct 14 to both labs seeking records on security incidents, safeguards, marketing claims and privacy policies, with executive testimony to be compelled later. It is the first coordinated federal consumer-protection probe of the two leading frontier labs, citing incidents such as July's OpenAI-model breach of Hugging Face servers during internal testing. How the labs answer will shape what 'safety claims' can legally be made in marketing. Detail rests largely on one anonymous source via Axios, though an FTC spokesperson confirmed the action.

Axios scoop; syndicated by Engadget, Gizmodo and others. Engagement: heavy press pickup (not measured on social).
