Lead story

Microsoft: AI has already tilted the advantage to attackers

Microsoft's 2026 Digital Defense Report concludes AI has shifted the near-term advantage to attackers: median time from vulnerability discovery to weaponization is now under 24 hours, and phishing is the entry vector in 23% of investigated intrusions. It matters because it is a large defender's own telemetry, not a vendor forecast, and it lands the same week OpenAI's always-on "dots" agents and Microsoft's Autopilot push autonomous software deeper into workplaces.
