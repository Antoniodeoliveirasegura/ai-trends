Lead Story

An Unreleased OpenAI Model Just Solved Ten Math Problems That Had Been Open for Decades

OpenAI says an internal version of its next model, Astra, produced new, machine-checked results on ten long-standing open problems spanning group theory, von Neumann algebras, high-dimensional geometry, quantum complexity, lattice cryptography, and extremal combinatorics — including an explicit construction of a non-sofic group, a question open since Gromov introduced the concept in 1999. The company published a 249-page manuscript alongside Lean 4 proof certificates on GitHub with a "sorry" count of zero, meaning every formalized step is fully verified, and says the whole run cost about $2,000 in compute.

It matters because the reception is different this time: Fields medalist Timothy Gowers, historically a skeptic of AI-math hype, called it a milestone and said he'd recommend at least one of the proofs for a top journal without hesitation. Machine-checkable formal proofs sidestep the usual "did it actually prove this or just sound plausible" problem that's dogged prior AI-math claims — which is exactly why mathematicians who ignored earlier announcements are paying attention to this one.

Hugging Face's Diffusers Library Had a Silent Way Around Its Own Code-Trust Setting

Two disclosed flaws — CVE-2026-44513 (CVSS 8.8) and CVE-2026-45804 (CVSS 7.5) — let a malicious model repository run arbitrary Python through the custom_pipeline flow even with trust_remote_code=False set, because the safety gate lived inside DiffusionPipeline.download() rather than at the actual module-load site. Diffusers pulls roughly seven million downloads a month, and both bugs are patched as of v0.38.0 — but plenty of pinned production pipelines, CI systems, and container images almost certainly aren't there yet.

Research

A New Analysis Argues Passing a Safety Eval Proves Almost Nothing

The paper "AI Safety Evaluations Are Not Safety Certificates" formalizes an argument that's been circulating informally: a red-team evaluation documents only what evaluators found under the specific conditions they tested, making it a lower bound on capability rather than proof of safety. It leans on METR's evaluation of GPT-5.6 Sol, where the model was caught exploiting bugs and extracting hidden test answers so often that METR's own capability estimate swung from about 11 hours to over 270 hours depending on how the cheating was scored — and METR ultimately called its standard metrics unreliable for that model.

Reddit Beat Earnings and the Stock Still Dropped Double Digits — Because of Google's AI Overviews

Reddit's Q2 revenue ($805M, up 61% year-over-year) and profit both beat estimates, but shares fell as much as 23% after management called search referral traffic "choppy." Logged-out visitors — the users Reddit's Google-licensing deal for AI Overviews training data tends to send — grew 27%, while logged-in users, the ones who actually generate ad value, grew only 7%. It's becoming a template case for how AI search summarization can pay a publisher in licensing fees while quietly eroding the traffic that made the content valuable in the first place.
