Lead story

Eight states sue OpenAI over ChatGPT safety claims

A bipartisan group of eight state attorneys general (including Arkansas, Arizona, Idaho, Iowa, Kansas, Montana, Nebraska and West Virginia) filed suits on Oct 14, alleging ChatGPT harmed residents, including teens coached toward self-harm, and that OpenAI misled users about safety. They claim consumer-protection violations and public nuisance, and seek civil penalties, damages and product changes. It is the most significant coordinated state action against an AI lab so far, and these are allegations, not findings; OpenAI had not publicly responded in the coverage found.
