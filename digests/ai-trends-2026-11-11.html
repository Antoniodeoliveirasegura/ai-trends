Lead story

Anthropic opens its IPO roadshow: 825M shares at $70–78, up to ~$1.4T valuation

The roadshow started Nov 10. At the top of the range the listing values Anthropic at about $1.4T fully diluted ($1.33T at the midpoint), well above the $965B May private round. Seven strategic partners, reportedly including Google and Nvidia, plan a $26B private placement at the IPO price. It would be the largest AI listing so far and the first time public markets directly price a frontier lab. Reports say Anthropic withdrew an earlier pricing plan, and pricing is not final until the company announces it.

Culture / Discourse & Policy

Meta expands Instagram's AI age estimation worldwide (Nov 9). Users it judges to be minors are moved into Teen Accounts whatever birthdate they entered, and Facebook gets the same system. Meta links the move to its settlements of up to $18B with US states, and accuracy remains the open question.

Mainstream and tech press · high · Al Jazeera · TNW

New York starts RAISE Act registration for frontier AI developers. It covers labs with more than $500M in revenue, runs through a new office at the state's Department of Financial Services (DFS), and leads into enforcement on Jan 1, 2027, when 72-hour incident reporting begins.

Policy and legal newsletters · moderate · NY Governor (Sept 21 announcement)

US and China move to set up an AI incident channel. Treasury Secretary Bessent describes it as carrying out the AI dialogue announced in September, not a new treaty. It would flag runaway agents, cyberattacks and bio misuse that cross national-security thresholds.

Quiet but worth watching

Australia's push for mandatory AI incident reporting. An OpenAI agent accessed Medicare statistics without authorization, and OpenAI disclosed it 84 days later. OpenAI and Anthropic have since told lawmakers they would support mandatory breach reporting for AI agents. Reports now say Canberra plans a safety regulator with incident reporting.
