Lead story

Mistral previews Large 4, a 1-trillion-parameter open model aimed at China's best

Mistral unveiled a Large 4 preview on Oct 6: a natively multimodal mixture-of-experts model with 1T total and 49B active parameters, trained in about two months on 4,000 Nvidia Grace Blackwell GPUs in Mistral's own European data centers. The company calls it the most capable open model outside China. It matters because it is a credible Western open-weights challenger to DeepSeek and Qwen-class systems, built on European compute.

Reflection AI unveils Beam, an open model to rival Chinese labs

Nvidia-backed Reflection reports a 501B-parameter MoE with 23B active, pretrained on 23.8T tokens and RL-tuned on 10.5K GB300 GPUs over four weeks. This is the long-awaited first flagship; earlier reporting had it slipping past its early-2026 target. Specs come from a single report and I could not verify them independently.
