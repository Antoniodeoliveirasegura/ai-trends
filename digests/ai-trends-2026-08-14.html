Apple Trains Its Own AI Model for China — With Alibaba's Help, and Beijing's Approval

Apple has quietly trained a proprietary large language model for the Chinese market with support from Alibaba, making it the first foreign company approved by Chinese regulators to offer its own AI model in the country. The "dual-track" arrangement pairs Apple's in-house model with Alibaba's Qwen, which will be woven into Apple Intelligence across iOS, iPadOS, macOS and visionOS for users in China, with rollout expected in the coming months.

It matters because it's a rare crack in the wall that's kept U.S. AI companies largely locked out of China — Apple is threading a path through Beijing's approval process while still leaning on a domestic partner for compliance and capability, a template rivals like Google and Microsoft don't currently have. It's also a defensive move: Apple Intelligence has lagged badly in China against Huawei, and this is Apple's clearest bid yet to close that gap before the iPhone loses more ground.
