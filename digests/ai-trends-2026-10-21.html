Lead story

Anthropic circulates a draft federal AI bill: block a model only for 50+ deaths or $1B+ damage, and only with a judge's sign-off

Confirmed by Anthropic after Politico's report, the draft would let Commerce seek a D.C. federal court order suspending a model only for imminent risk of killing or seriously injuring 50+ people or over $1B in damage, limited to bioweapon, loss-of-control and cyberattack scenarios. It also adds registered independent evaluators with a 72-hour duty to report serious risks. It matters because a leading lab is trying to set the shape of US frontier-model rules before Congress, out of session until after the midterms, acts. Caveat: it is a proposal, first reported around Oct 17, and still the day's most-discussed policy item.
