Research

"Never Give Up": A Fix for RL's Blind Spot on Hard Math and Coding Problems

Michael Noukhovitch, Hamish Ivison, Nathan Lambert, and Aaron Courville (Mila/AI2) name and attack the "Matthew Effect" in RL post-training: models get much better at problems they can already mostly solve, and barely improve on the hard ones. Their method, NGU, keeps resampling a prompt asynchronously until it gets one correct rollout, automatically shifting compute away from easy prompts and toward the hard ones that actually need it.
