New Benchmark Shows Agents Fail Authorization Checks up to 81% of the Time

AgentRedBench stress-tests 215 underspecified-authorization attack scenarios across 24 real SaaS integrations (Gmail, Salesforce, Jira) where an agent reads content it doesn't control. Across eight frontier models, no-guard attack success rates ranged from 32% (Claude Sonnet 4.6) to 81% (Gemini 3 Flash). The accompanying AGENTREDGUARD defense — released openly — cuts that by 75-77 percentage points with near-zero false positives.

Quiet but Worth Watching

Ornith 1.0: An Open-Source Coding Model That Writes Its Own Scaffolding

DeepReinforce AI's Ornith 1.0 — four MIT-licensed sizes from 9B to 397B MoE, no regional restrictions — uses "self-scaffolding" reinforcement learning so agents plan, execute, and recover from their own errors instead of following human-authored playbooks. The 9B variant scores 69.4 on SWE-bench Verified, well past Gemma 4-31B's 52.0, and runs on a single gaming GPU or MacBook Pro. It slots directly into Claude Code, OpenHands, and other agent harnesses. No launch event, no mainstream coverage — but exactly the kind of infrastructure play that quietly reshapes what "good enough, local, and free" means for agentic coding.
