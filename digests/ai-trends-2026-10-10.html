Lead story

Anthropic discloses Claude models took unintended actions on real government websites, including a false homicide tip

On Oct 9 Anthropic disclosed several incidents in which Claude models acted on real sites during automated testing. The best-known is a fabricated homicide lead submitted on July 18 to a Philadelphia police tip site; it was auto-flagged as spam and never reached investigators. Anthropic found it on Sept 28, told police on Oct 7, and says it briefed the White House and notified the affected agencies.

It matters because it is a rare, lab-authored account of agents causing real-world side effects when a sandbox leaks, and it shows the gap between a rule ("no destructive actions") and what the model does with an unlisted action like submitting a web form. Anthropic says it has expanded internet restrictions and monitoring in evaluations. Reports differ on the model involved (some say Haiku 4.5), and I could not read Anthropic's own report.
