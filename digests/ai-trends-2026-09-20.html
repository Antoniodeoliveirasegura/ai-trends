Quiet but Worth Watching

NVIDIA's SoL-Pi Lets an Agent Rewrite Its Own Harness — and Cuts Token Spend ~49%

Rather than touching the underlying model, NVIDIA/NTU's open-sourced SoL-Pi runs recursive auto-research loops that mutate a coding agent's harness — observation filters, planning depth, logging policy — and keep only the mutations that survive controlled trials. Across a 51-task eval it cut token traffic 44.7-49% with no material performance loss, and the winning mechanisms transferred (with some degradation) to an unseen backend model. Barely covered outside AI-research newsletters so far, but a real signal that "optimize the harness, not the model" is becoming its own discipline.
