Quiet but Worth Watching

A Quiet Paper Pokes a Hole in "Self-Improving Agent" Reproducibility

"On the Fragility of Self-Improving Agents: Variance, Task Order, and Underspecification," posted to arXiv today by Qinyuan Ye, Yu Li, Yada Pruksachatkun, Jiaxin Zhang, and Chien-Sheng Wu, re-runs popular memory-based self-improving agent methods — the ones that build a textual memory bank from a task stream to get better over time — across multiple trials and shuffled task orders. The methods show high run-to-run variance and real sensitivity to task ordering, undercutting reproducibility claims in a subfield that's gotten a lot of hype lately. No launch event, just a careful reproducibility check with code and data released alongside it.
