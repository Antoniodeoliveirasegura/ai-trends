Lead story

ChatGPT gets GPT-6 and "Intelligent UI" — answers that are interfaces, not text

OpenAI rolled out GPT-6 with Intelligent UI on Oct 7 (paid tiers; Free and Go followed Oct 8), letting ChatGPT reply with interactive diagrams, charts, buttons, forms and calculators instead of only prose. Paid plans run GPT-6 Sol and Free/Go run GPT-6 Luna; it applies to Chat only, not Work or Codex.

It matters because it shifts the chatbot from a text box to a generative front end, reaching free users on day two. It also follows September's criticized "messy" GPT-6 Astra rollout, and independent reviews and benchmarks of the feature are not yet out, so early impressions lean on OpenAI's own materials.
