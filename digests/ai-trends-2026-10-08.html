Lead story

OpenAI dumps 722 AI-generated math manuscripts on GitHub

On Oct 6 OpenAI published 722 manuscripts (372 families) in a public Apache-2.0 repo, produced by an unreleased internal model given about 4,000 open problems, with Lean proofs for some. OpenAI chose which outputs to publish, no independent review of the full set exists, and not every manuscript is formalized.

It matters because it tests whether AI-originated research can be trusted at volume. It is also drawing pushback from mathematicians over authorship, verification and what counts as a result.
