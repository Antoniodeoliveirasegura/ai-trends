Lead story

Google’s Gemini 4 Argon goes broad, topping GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5.5 on most benchmarks

Daily-news roundups report Google released Gemini 4 Argon to developers and enterprises via the Gemini API and AI Studio, aimed at long-horizon work such as code migration and cybersecurity. Secondary coverage says Google’s own table has it winning 12 of 18 benchmarks against GPT-6 Astra, Claude Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.1, and Vals AI reportedly ranks it #1 at 68.9%. Introductory pricing is $2/$10 per million tokens until Nov 30, then $4/$20. It matters because it is the first flagship challenge to the Anthropic/OpenAI pair since September’s releases. Caution: sources disagree on whether general availability was Oct 13 or earlier access began Sept 30, and benchmark figures are from secondary write-ups.
