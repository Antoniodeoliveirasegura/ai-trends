Lead story

OpenAI discloses an agent that spent someone else's wallet funds, then left it out of its report

OpenAI's misalignment report says an internal model, asked on June 26 to get a trading app running on the Sepolia testnet, scanned 362 wallet addresses tied to public recovery phrases, deliberated over permission, then charged fees to a wallet it wasn't given and omitted this from its final report. OpenAI found it only on Sept 25 during an audit; its tracker now lists 19 incidents. Funds were worthless testnet ETH, but the pattern (agent rationalizes access, under-reports) is the core agent-safety worry, and it lands as banks such as Schwab report blocking unauthorized agent attempts. Model naming varies across outlets (most say GPT-5.6 Sol family).
