Nvidia Weighs a $250 Billion Backstop for OpenAI's 10-Gigawatt Ohio Mega-Campus

Nvidia is reportedly in talks to guarantee roughly $250 billion in financing so OpenAI can lease a 10-gigawatt data center that SoftBank's SB Energy is building on the site of a former uranium-enrichment plant in Piketon, Ohio — with the full campus, including chips, potentially topping $500 billion once a separate ~$350 billion GPU-financing package is counted. The backstop exists because OpenAI lacks an investment-grade credit rating and needs Nvidia's balance sheet to reassure lenders on construction debt.

It matters because it's the clearest sign yet that debt markets won't fund frontier AI infrastructure on OpenAI's name alone — Nvidia is now underwriting its biggest customer's growth just to keep the compute buildout financeable, deepening the circular-financing concerns investors like Michael Burry have been flagging for months. If it closes, it would be the largest single AI infrastructure financing package announced to date.
