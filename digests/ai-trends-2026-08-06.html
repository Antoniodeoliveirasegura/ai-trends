Lead Story

Google DeepMind's Hassabis Steps Back as CEO — and Jeff Dean Quits After 27 Years to Start a Rival Research Lab

Google shook up its AI leadership on August 5: Demis Hassabis is moving from CEO of DeepMind into a new Chair of Google DeepMind / Chief Scientist of Alphabet role, while CTO Koray Kavukcuoglu is elevated to SVP of Google DeepMind, reporting directly to Sundar Pichai and taking direct ownership of Gemini model development, frontier research, and the Gemini app. Google shares fell nearly 4% on the news.

It matters because the reshuffle landed the same day Jeff Dean — Google's chief scientist for 27 years — announced he's leaving alongside senior fellow Sanjay Ghemawat, DeepMind VP Oriol Vinyals, and Google Brain co-founder Quoc Le to found Discovery Loop, a public-benefit corporation aimed at automating machine-learning and scientific research. Google itself is a founding investor in the new venture, an unusually candid admission that its own leadership churn is happening while Gemini's flagship model remains unreleased months past a planned June launch, and while Anthropic and OpenAI keep setting the pace.

White House Finalizes Its Frontier-AI Vetting Framework — Then Declines to Publish It

The administration briefed OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Microsoft, and Nvidia on August 4 on a voluntary 30-day early-access framework for cybersecurity review of closed-source frontier models — but has no plans to release the framework's text publicly, and it excludes open-weight models entirely. Critics say the secrecy plus the open/closed carve-out creates a structural asymmetry over which labs actually face oversight.

Quiet but Worth Watching

OpenAI Disrupted a Cambodia-Based Scam Compound Built Entirely on ChatGPT

OpenAI banned a coordinated network of ChatGPT accounts tied to Cambodia's Preah Sihanouk province — a region tied to trafficking-run scam compounds — after finding they were used to write and translate romance, investment, and gambling scam scripts, and even to manage trafficked workers' pay, debts, and visa status. It got a fraction of today's headline attention, but it's one of the most detailed public looks yet at AI-enabled scam infrastructure operating at industrial scale, disclosed by OpenAI itself.
