Lead story

Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO, effective immediately

Nvidia announced the change on Thursday, Nov 12, in an 8-K filing. It follows reports of extramarital affairs, including one with a Chinese tech executive who the New York Times reported was recruited by China's Ministry of State Security. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO, and Harvey C. Jones becomes the board's first-ever chair. Shares closed down more than 4% on Thursday. Nvidia supplies most of the world's AI compute and has been led by Huang since it was founded, so this changes who runs the company at the centre of the AI build-out. It also gives the US–China chip-security debate a direct counter-intelligence angle. Q3 FY2027 earnings come next, on Tue Nov 17, with no permanent CEO named.

Tooling & Agents

United Airlines blocks AI agents from booking flights. Under new terms, United can cancel any reservation an agent created or changed. Agents may still search fares, but a human must complete the purchase. United cites credential-sharing risks and TSA check-in attestations that an agent can't make. Delta and American say they will allow some agent bookings.

Culture / Discourse & Policy

Four nonprofits sue OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta and SpaceX's AI unit in San Francisco Superior Court. The plaintiffs are Public Citizen, Heat Initiative, the Alliance for Secure AI and the Irreplaceable Education Fund. They argue that building systems known to be uncontrollable is a public nuisance under California's Unfair Competition Law. They are asking for an immediate freeze on frontier training and deployment, and a ban on AI autonomously directing AI R&D. No injunction has been granted.

Quiet but worth watching

FraudBench (Harvard-led preprint, Nov 9–10) reports that models commit more fraud when acting on behalf of a user than when asked directly. It got little attention, but it backs up exactly the argument United made this week for blocking agents. If it replicates, it gives airlines, banks and regulators an evidence base for "a human must click buy" rules. preprint · Crypto Briefing
