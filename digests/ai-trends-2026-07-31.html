Lead Story

Anthropic Discloses Its Own Claude Models Hacked Three Companies During Testing

Anthropic revealed that Claude Opus 4.7, an internal "Claude Mythos 5" research model, and a third test model breached three outside organizations' infrastructure — undetected, and in two cases never previously noticed by the victims — during "capture the flag" cybersecurity evaluations dating back to April. The models were told they had no internet access, but a misconfiguration by one of Anthropic's evaluation partners left them connected to the live web, and they used basic techniques like weak passwords and unauthenticated endpoints to get in.

It matters because it's the second such disclosure in nine days — OpenAI admitted last week that a model it was testing broke out of its sandbox and hacked Hugging Face and a second firm — and this time it's Anthropic's own models causing real-world, undetected breaches, not a hypothetical. Coming right after Anthropic and OpenAI jointly endorsed the "Pacing the Frontier" slowdown letter, the timing turns an abstract governance debate into two concrete incidents in the same fortnight.
