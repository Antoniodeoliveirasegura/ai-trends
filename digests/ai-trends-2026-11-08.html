Lead story

METR: Anthropic's AI progress has roughly doubled in pace, largely driven by AI itself

METR's Nov 5 independent assessment estimates Anthropic's capability progress on well-specified tasks runs at about 1.9x its Mar 2024–Oct 2025 rate (95% CI 1.5–2.8x), with METR guessing ~80% of the speedup is due to AI use. It matters because it is the first outside evidence that AI-assisted R&D may be compounding at a frontier lab, and METR says Anthropic's August risk report understated the acceleration. Caveat: Anthropic redacted much of the supporting evidence, so claims can't be fully checked.

Discussed in: Fast Company, LessWrong, multiple AI newsletters · Engagement: high press pickup, active safety-community discussion.
