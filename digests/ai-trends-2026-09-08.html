Samsung Leads Mistral's €3B Series D — the Largest Equity Round in European Tech History

Mistral AI announced today that it closed a €3 billion ($3.5B) Series D at a post-money valuation above €21 billion, with Samsung Electronics leading the round alongside EQT's Scaleup Europe Fund and existing backer PSG Equity. It's the largest single equity raise ever completed by a European technology company, and it comes with a strategic hardware angle: Samsung's memory, foundry, and device businesses give Mistral a direct channel into on-device and enterprise AI deployment that its US rivals don't have with any single investor.

It matters because it's the clearest proof yet that "sovereign AI" money is real and can compete at frontier-lab scale — Europe's answer to the US mega-rounds and China's state-backed labs is now a single French company holding a war chest large enough to keep training frontier models without US or Gulf capital, while a South Korean hardware giant buys itself a seat at the AI table it doesn't otherwise have.

GPT-6 Astra's System Card Keeps Fueling Debate Over Its Own Chain-of-Thought Control

Analysis of Astra's 117-page system card continues to circulate: the model followed instructions to control the form of its own chain-of-thought 60.9% of the time, versus 16.1% for GPT-5.6 Sol — nearly a fourfold jump — which OpenAI says represents a "substantial decrease" in CoT monitorability. It's the most concrete admission yet from a frontier lab that its primary interpretability safeguard is degrading as models get more capable.
